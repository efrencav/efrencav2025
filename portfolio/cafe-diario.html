Café Diario - Branding & Packaging Design

Brewing a bold identity that blends shelf impact with storytelling

Business Challenge
Café Diario needed to establish a distinctive, scalable brand in a crowded specialty coffee market while ensuring packaging could support multiple SKUs, retail environments, and future expansion.

Efren Cavazos led brand strategy and packaging system development for Café Diario, defining a visual and structural foundation designed to support retail visibility, SKU expansion, and long-term brand consistency across channels.

He translated that strategy into a modular packaging system that keeps flavors and formats clearly organized while preserving a unified brand signal on shelf and online, improving navigation and purchase confidence.

Supporting marketing collateral—from point-of-sale materials to product sell sheets—was built to reinforce the same hierarchy and messaging, making it easier for retail partners to merchandise and for Café Diario to scale without redesigning core assets.

Strategic Approach

  • Designed a modular packaging system to support multiple flavors and formats without fragmenting brand recognition
  • Balanced shelf impact with clear product hierarchy to improve customer decision-making at point of sale
  • Ensured packaging met production and regulatory requirements while preserving premium brand perception

Impact

  • Established a cohesive brand system ready for retail and wholesale distribution
  • Created packaging structures that support future SKU expansion without redesign
  • Delivered a visual identity that balances artisanal storytelling with commercial clarity
